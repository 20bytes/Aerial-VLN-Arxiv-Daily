Finite-temperature Green's function cluster expansion from thermofield doubles: Breakdown of the polaron picture

Abstract (EN)

We introduce a method, numerically exact in principle, for computing the momentum- and frequency-resolved single-particle Green's function of a polaron at finite temperature. The method, which we refer to as the finite-temperature Green's function cluster expansion, combines two ingredients: the generalized Green's function cluster expansion, a numerically exact extension of the momentum average family of methods that solves the polaron problem at zero temperature through a hierarchy of equations of motion for restricted phonon cloud configurations; and the thermofield double formalism, which maps the thermal trace onto a pure-state expectation value over a doubled Hilbert space. The resulting equations of motion have the same algebraic structure as those of the multi-boson zero-temperature theory, with the temperature entering through a Bogoliubov-type mixing angle that controls the coupling to a set of fictitious bath bosons. We implement the method in our open-source software package and benchmark it on the one-dimensional Holstein polaron, computing spectral functions, dispersions, lifetimes, and effective masses across coupling regimes and temperatures up to $T/Ω\sim 1$. Where finite-temperature density matrix renormalization group results are available, we find quantitative agreement at affordable computational cost. The method recovers momentum-resolved spectra directly in frequency space, with no time evolution or analytic continuation. We also discuss the practical costs of the approach. In particular, since the doubled phonon Hilbert space has a non-trivial configuration structure in which real and fictitious clouds compete, convergence in the corresponding cloud parameters requires care.

摘要 (ZH)

我们提出了一种原则上数值精确的方法，用于计算极化子在有限温度下的动量与频率分辨的单粒子格林函数。该方法被称为有限温度格林函数团簇展开，它结合了两个要素：广义格林函数团簇展开，这是动量平均方法族的一种数值精确扩展，通过受限声子云构型的运动方程层级在零温下求解极化子问题；以及热场双重形式，它将热迹映射为加倍希尔伯特空间上的纯态期望值。由此产生的运动方程与多玻色子零温理论的代数结构相同，温度通过一个类似Bogoliubov的混合角进入，该角度控制与一组虚构热库玻色子的耦合。我们在开源软件包中实现了该方法，并在一维Holstein极化子上进行基准测试，计算了不同耦合区间和温度高达$T/Ω\sim 1$的光谱函数、色散、寿命和有效质量。在与有限温度密度矩阵重整化群结果可比较的情况下，我们以可负担的计算成本获得了定量一致的结果。该方法直接在频率空间中恢复动量分辨光谱，无需时间演化或解析延拓。我们还讨论了该方法的实际成本。特别是，由于加倍声子希尔伯特空间具有非平凡的构型结构，其中真实和虚构的云相互竞争，相应云参数的收敛需要谨慎处理。
