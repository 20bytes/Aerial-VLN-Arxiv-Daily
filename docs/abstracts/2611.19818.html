Drone Navigation for Fast, Safe Emergency Response

Abstract (EN)

In 2025, there was a total of 358 weather-related disasters worldwide affecting millions of people. For unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) to autonomously aid these people, they need to be able to navigate around complex obstacles and reach their destinations to deliver supplies or quickly locate survivors in disaster-stricken areas. Our research addresses this need by providing a method to compute minimum-time trajectories in real time while providing guarantees of obstacle avoidance along the trajectory. Previous approaches treat the problem of computing time-optimal trajectories without considering obstacle avoidance. Our work not only includes obstacle avoidance constraints, but also improves overall mission time for navigation. We define mission time as the sum of planning time and traversal time. In time-sensitive emergencies, rapid trajectory planning is essential to ensure the prompt delivery of critical medical aid. As such, we consider the combined planning and traversal time as the key metric for how quickly the vehicle can reach a survivor. We show a sample mission in Fig. 1 where the quadrotor navigates through multiple canyon-like obstacles to deliver aid; during most of this trajectory, it is applying the near-maximum thrust.

摘要 (ZH)

2025年，全球共发生358起与天气相关的灾害，影响数百万人。为使无人机（UAV）能够自主援助这些人群，它们需要能够在复杂障碍物周围导航并到达目的地，以运送物资或在受灾地区快速定位幸存者。我们的研究通过提供一种实时计算最短时间轨迹的方法来满足这一需求，同时保证沿轨迹避开障碍物。先前的方法在处理计算时间最优轨迹问题时未考虑避障。我们的工作不仅包含避障约束，还改善了导航的整体任务时间。我们将任务时间定义为规划时间与遍历时间之和。在时间敏感的紧急情况下，快速轨迹规划对于确保关键医疗援助的及时送达至关重要。因此，我们将规划与遍历时间之和作为衡量飞行器多快能到达幸存者的关键指标。我们在图1中展示了一个示例任务，其中四旋翼飞行器穿越多个峡谷状障碍物以运送援助；在该轨迹的大部分过程中，它都在施加接近最大的推力。
