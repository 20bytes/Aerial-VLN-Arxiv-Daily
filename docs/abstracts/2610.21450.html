X-Fly: Learning to Reach and Stop in Full-Chunk Aerial Vision-Language Navigation

Abstract (EN)

Aerial vision-language navigation (VLN) agents built on large vision-language models are expensive to query. Executing a complete predicted action chunk before replanning amortizes this cost, reducing model inference from 195 to 42 ms per executed waypoint for our policy. It also moves the states at which the policy is queried away from expert demonstrations, which supervise neither recovery from these states nor termination at the learner's own arrival. On OpenFly test-seen, no-stop failures account for 47.78% of full-chunk behavior cloning (BC) outcomes. We present X-Fly, a staged training framework for full-chunk aerial VLN. Recovery DAgger labels learner-visited decision states with smooth chunks that reconnect to future expert segments and with STOP at the first in-region state. Flow-GRPO then jointly refines the chunked flow controller and a Bernoulli STOP policy from same-task closed-loop outcomes, supplemented by outcome-verified stopping supervision. With eight controls per decision, X-Fly reaches 74.18% SR on test-seen and 51.73% on test-unseen, compared with 16.45% and 18.43% for full-chunk BC and 32.04% and 29.46% for DreamFly.

摘要 (ZH)

基于大型视觉语言模型的空中视觉语言导航（VLN）智能体查询成本高昂。在执行完整预测动作块后再重新规划可分摊此成本，将我们策略每执行一个航路点的模型推理时间从195毫秒减少到42毫秒。这也使得策略查询的状态偏离专家演示，而专家演示既未监督从这些状态恢复，也未监督在学习者自身到达时终止。在OpenFly测试可见集上，无停止失败占完整块行为克隆（BC）结果的47.78%。我们提出X-Fly，一个用于完整块空中VLN的分阶段训练框架。恢复DAgger用平滑块标记学习者访问的决策状态，这些块重新连接到未来专家片段，并在第一个区域内状态处标记停止。Flow-GRPO然后从同任务闭环结果中联合优化分块流控制器和伯努利停止策略，并辅以结果验证的停止监督。每个决策有八个控制，X-Fly在测试可见集上达到74.18%的成功率，在测试未见集上达到51.73%，而完整块BC分别为16.45%和18.43%，DreamFly分别为32.04%和29.46%。
