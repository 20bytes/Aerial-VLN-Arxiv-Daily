QED corrections to neutron and nuclear $β$-decay at order $α^2$

Abstract (EN)

We compute two-loop QED corrections to neutron and nuclear beta decay, including full electron mass dependence and real radiation contributions. The result for the neutron, $δ_{R,\,\rm static}= ( 29.199 \pm 0.001 )\times 10^{-3}$, implies a factor $\sim 70 $ reduction in the uncertainty of the low-energy radiative corrections compared to previous results obtained in the large energy/small electron-mass limit, $m_e/(m_n-m_p) \to 0$. For superallowed nuclear decays we find that the $Z$-independent order $α^2$ corrections range from $(0.1-0.5) \times 10^{-4}$ i.e., of roughly the expected size and comparable to current uncertainty estimates.

摘要 (ZH)

我们计算了中子与核β衰变的两圈QED修正，包含完整的电子质量依赖和实辐射贡献。中子结果 δ_{R, static} = (29.199 ± 0.001) × 10^{-3}，意味着与先前在大能量/小电子质量极限 m_e/(m_n-m_p) → 0 下获得的结果相比，低能辐射修正的不确定度减少了约70倍。对于超允许核衰变，我们发现与Z无关的α²阶修正范围在(0.1-0.5) × 10^{-4}，即大致符合预期大小，并与当前的不确定度估计相当。
