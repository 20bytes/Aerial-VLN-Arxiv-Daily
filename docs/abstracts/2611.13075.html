Shaping black hole resonances II: Spectroscopic landscapes and interference in black hole ringdown

Abstract (EN)

We map analytically the excitation of Schwarzschild quasinormal modes (QNMs) $C_n$ by oscillatory Gaussian perturbations across the pulse width $σ$ and carrier frequency $ν$. The excitation coefficient of each mode is determined by the product of an intrinsic excitation factor and a source-dependent overlap evaluated at the complex QNM frequency. For oscillatory Gaussian perturbations, this overlap is the coherent sum of the positive- and negative-frequency branches of the source, with its amplitude factorizing exactly into a Gaussian spectral envelope and an interference factor. We observe that the excitation amplitude peaks at $ν=ω_n^{\rm Re}$ only for narrowband sources. For sufficiently broadband pulses, the maximum lies at $ν=0$, so efficient excitation does not necessarily require matching the carrier and the mode frequency. We observe that the interference between branches, modulates the amplitude only for nonzero $ν$, a feature generic to real oscillatory sources. The prominence of the interference-induced modulation is controlled by the ratio $ω_n^{\rm Re}/ω_n^{\rm Im}$. The phase separates into a mode-only, a width-only and a $ν$-dependent term, the last generated by the same interference, with $ν=0$ being its unique maximum at fixed width. The analytical predictions are validated against time-domain numerical simulations, showing a relative median error of $\sim 4\%$ for $|C_0|$. For $|C_1|$, the median error is $\sim 16\%$ when $σ\leq 5$, increasing to $\sim 36\%$ across the full parameter space. The median phase difference for $n=0$ is $\sim0.14 \,\rm rad $ and for $n=1$, $σ\leq5$ it is $0.34\,\rm rad$. Our results demonstrate that QNM excitation cannot be understood through frequency matching alone and may provide qualitative insights into more realistic astrophysical scenarios.

摘要 (ZH)

我们通过解析方法，映射了振荡高斯微扰在脉冲宽度 σ 和载波频率 ν 范围内对史瓦西准正规模（QNMs）$C_n$ 的激发。每个模式的激发系数由内禀激发因子与在复QNM频率处评估的源相关重叠的乘积决定。对于振荡高斯微扰，该重叠是源正频率分支和负频率分支的相干叠加，其幅度精确分解为高斯谱包络和干涉因子。我们观察到，仅对于窄带源，激发幅度在 $ν=ω_n^{ m Re}$ 处达到峰值。对于足够宽带的脉冲，最大值位于 $ν=0$，因此有效激发不一定需要载波与模式频率匹配。我们观察到，分支间的干涉仅在非零 ν 时调制幅度，这是实振荡源的普遍特征。干涉引起的调制显著程度由比值 $ω_n^{ m Re}/ω_n^{ m Im}$ 控制。相位分解为仅模式项、仅宽度项和依赖于 ν 的项，最后一项由相同的干涉产生，在固定宽度下 ν=0 是其唯一最大值。解析预测经时域数值模拟验证，对于 |C_0|，相对中值误差约为 4%。对于 |C_1|，当 σ≤5 时中值误差约为 16%，在整个参数空间中增至约 36%。n=0 的中值相位差约为 0.14 弧度，n=1 且 σ≤5 时为 0.34 弧度。我们的结果表明，QNM 激发不能仅通过频率匹配来理解，并可能为更现实的天体物理场景提供定性见解。
