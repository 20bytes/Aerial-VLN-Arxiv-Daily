The uncertainty operator, the Weil ground state and the zeros of zeta: a numerical study and conditional positivity of the Weil quadratic form

Abstract (EN)

Applying the Dirichlet principle to the Weil quadratic form $QW_λ$ on $L^2([λ^{-1},λ],d^*u)$--using only the primes $p\leλ^2$ of the Euler product--yields canonical finite-dimensional Hermitian matrices whose ground state has a Mellin transform with zeros proved real in full generality when the lowest eigenvalue is simple and even. While $λ^2=13$ gave $50$ zeros of $ζ$, $λ^2=250$ ($λ\sim16$) gives the first $1000$ zeros with $>500$ correct decimals (the first with $1341$). We explain this via a correspondence between $QW_λ$ and Slepian's time-frequency uncertainty operator $U_λ=2-P_λ-\widehat P_λ$, diagonalized by prolate wave functions with eigenvalues $δ_n(λ)\sim c_nλ^{2n+1}e^{-4πλ^2}$ (Fuchs). Via $\mathcal{E}(f)(u)=u^{1/2}\sum_{n\ge1}f(nu)$ and Gevrey smoothing, $ε_λ\le Cδ_4(λ)(\logλ)^3$ is a theorem (Connes-Kastoris). Computations up to $λ^2=300$ ($ε_λ(1)\sim10^{-1618}$) show a striking logarithmic coincidence with the Fuchs ladder, confirming this simplicity and evenness. Prolate vectors reproduce all minuscule eigenvectors, and conditioning by them and the pole term removes the minuscule spectrum: $QW_λ$ is a direct sum of a finite-dimensional near-radical part (Fuchs ladder) and an infinite-dimensional positive part from the Sonin space ($U_λ=2$). Under this ladder hypothesis and $QW_λ\ge0$, we bound the ground-state distance to the target with Mellin transform $\frac14ξ(s+\frac12)$ (Hurwitz' theorem). Finally, the semilocal trace formula gives the uncertainty principle via Coburn's lemma and proves, under a hypothesis valid at $\infty$, that $QW_λ\ge c>0$ outside a finite-dimensional near-self-dual space, reducing Weil positivity to a Schur complement at the ladder's edge.

摘要 (ZH)

将Dirichlet原理应用于$L^2([λ^{-1},λ],d^*u)$上的Weil二次型$QW_λ$——仅使用Euler积中$p\leλ^2$的素数——可得到典范的有限维Hermite矩阵，当最低特征值单且为偶时，其基态的Mellin变换的零点在完全一般性下被证明为实。当$λ^2=13$时给出了$ζ$的$50$个零点，而$λ^2=250$（$λ\sim16$）给出了前$1000$个零点，精度超过$500$位正确小数（首个达到$1341$位）。我们通过$QW_λ$与Slepian时频不确定性算子$U_λ=2-P_λ-\widehat P_λ$之间的对应来解释这一点，该算子由长球波函数对角化，特征值为$δ_n(λ)\sim c_nλ^{2n+1}e^{-4πλ^2}$（Fuchs）。通过$\mathcal{E}(f)(u)=u^{1/2}\sum_{n\ge1}f(nu)$和Gevrey光滑化，$ε_λ\le Cδ_4(λ)(\logλ)^3$是一个定理（Connes-Kastoris）。计算至$λ^2=300$（$ε_λ(1)\sim10^{-1618}$）显示出与Fuchs阶梯惊人的对数巧合，证实了这种单性和偶性。长球向量重现了所有极小特征向量，并且通过它们和极点项进行条件化消除了极小谱：$QW_λ$是一个有限维近根部分（Fuchs阶梯）与来自Sonin空间（$U_λ=2$）的无限维正部分的直和。在此阶梯假设和$QW_λ\ge0$下，我们界定了基态到目标的距离，其Mellin变换为$\frac14ξ(s+\frac12)$（Hurwitz定理）。最后，半局部迹公式通过Coburn引理给出了不确定性原理，并在一个在$\infty$处成立的假设下证明了在有限维近自对偶空间之外$QW_λ\ge c>0$，将Weil正性归结为阶梯边缘处的Schur补。
